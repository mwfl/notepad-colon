Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

Notepad:: is a local-first Windows text and code editor. It does not collect, store, sell, or transmit personal information.

Data collection

Notepad:: does not include telemetry, analytics, advertising, user accounts, cloud synchronization, or automatic update checks. The application does not send information about you, your device, or your documents to the developer or to third parties.

Your files and settings

Documents are opened and saved only when you direct the application to do so. Editing, search, recovery, session, and settings data are processed and stored locally on your device. Notepad:: does not upload document contents or local settings.

Network access

Notepad:: does not require an internet connection for its editing features and does not perform background network communication. Microsoft Store may independently provide installation and update services under Microsoft's own terms and privacy policy.

Changes to this policy

If the application's privacy behavior changes, this policy will be updated before a release containing that change. The effective date above identifies the latest revision.

Contact

Questions or privacy concerns may be submitted through the Notepad:: issue tracker.
